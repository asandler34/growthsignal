Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026.

GrowthSignal is operated by Granite Coast Ventures, LLC, 27 Rock Orchard, Rye, NH 03870, USA ("we"). This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Free scans: the website address you enter, and optionally a business name, type, town and services. A hashed form of your IP address for abuse limits.
  • Accounts: your email address, the sites and business facts you add, your reports and AI answer samples.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers and plan status, never your card number.
  • Messages: what you send through our contact form.

What we do with it

We use it to run scans, take answer samples, send the emails you need (sign in links, report updates, billing notices), respond to messages and prevent abuse. We do not sell personal information and do not use your data to advertise to you.

Who processes it

Our hosting and database provider, Stripe for payments, our email delivery provider, and the AI platforms we query (currently OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity). AI platforms receive the questions we ask on your behalf, which may include your business name and town. They do not receive your email address.

Public website content

Scans read public pages of the website you enter. We store extracted facts and short excerpts as evidence in your report.

Retention

Free reports not saved to an account are deleted after 30 days. Account reports are kept for your plan's history period. When you delete your account, your sites, reports and samples are deleted. Billing records may be kept by Stripe as required by law.

Your choices

You can turn off monitoring emails and delete your account at any time from your dashboard. To request access or deletion of other data, use the message form on our homepage.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.
