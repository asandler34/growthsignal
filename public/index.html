05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
Before you pay.

Can GrowthSignal get me recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee that. Recommendations depend on the question, the platform, location, reviews and other sites. We fix what is in your control on your website, show you which outside sources AI relies on for your questions, and measure what happens over time.

Why not just ask ChatGPT myself?

You should, and it is free. One answer from one app is a single snapshot. We ask several realistic questions on several platforms, repeat them, record every source, and track changes month to month, which is hard to do by hand.

Do your AI samples match what my customers see?

Not exactly. We use official APIs with web search turned on. Consumer apps can personalize answers, use different settings and change without notice. We label every sample with the platform, model, location given and time, and treat results as a sample, not a ranking.

Is this just SEO?

There is a lot of overlap: accessible pages, clear services and accurate business facts help in both. We add the AI specific parts: which AI crawlers you block, whether AI answers mention you, and which sources they cite.

Do you change my website?

Never. We generate changes for you to review and paste in yourself, or hand to your web person. Then we rescan to confirm they are live.

What does the free scan collect?

Your website address and, if you add them, your business name and town. We read public pages on your site. Free reports without an account are deleted after 30 days. See the privacy policy.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from Plan and billing in your dashboard. Paid features stay on until the end of the period you paid for, then your account returns to the free plan and your history is kept for the free plan's retention.
