### Notes
- **Cheapest credible incumbent option for a single-location local business: BrightLocal Track at $31/mo (annual) / $41/mo (monthly).** It is local-first, location-level, and includes a GBP audit, citations, and rank tracking. Since Sept 2026 it is a near-direct substitute for a GrowthSignal-style local AI tracker. Its limits: 3 engines, no Perplexity or Gemini, checks twice a month, 20 prompts.
- Next cheapest: HubSpot AEO $45-50/mo (no local or geo-grid focus), Ahrefs custom prompts $50/mo (needs prompt design), SE Visible $79/mo, Semrush AI Visibility and Similarweb at $99/mo.
- Website builders (Wix, Squarespace) already bundle basic AI visibility checks for their own customers, which shrinks the market of "my site is on Wix or Squarespace, how am I doing in ChatGPT".
- Yext and Birdeye say outright that they target enterprise multi-location brands, so single-location SMBs are left to resellers and agencies.
- Conflicts noted: Semrush engine coverage and base-plan requirement differ across third-party sources, and the Semrush pricing page was CAPTCHA-blocked. Confirm before citing externally.

### How much of GrowthSignal's value can a motivated owner replicate for free? (analyst judgment, not a sourced fact)
- **Snapshot diagnosis, about 70-80% replicable in 1-2 hours.** Run 2-3 free graders, ask 10 prompts in each of 3 assistants, run Rich Results Test and the schema validator, read robots.txt, and check the GBP. The free graders already cover crawler access, schema, and competitor mentions.
- **Ongoing tracking, about 20-30% replicable.** Repeating 10-20 prompts across 3-4 engines weekly and logging them in a spreadsheet takes roughly 1-2 hours a week, with noisy, non-comparable results. GSC and Bing give partial first-party signals (impressions and citations, no clicks), and GSC's report is not available to every site yet.
- **Prioritized local fixes, mostly not replicable for free.** Knowing which citation sources (GBP, Yelp, BBB, Angi) matter for your category and city, what to change, and in what order is the gap. Free graders produce generic advice. BrightLocal ($31/mo) now sells exactly this, so "free" is not the only competitor.

## Part 3: Demand and context evidence

Labels: **[Fact]** means third-party or survey methodology disclosed, **[Vendor stat]** means a vendor's own research or claim (a potential bias), **[Not found]** means no data found today.

### Consumer use of AI for local discovery
1. **[Vendor stat, survey n=1,002 US adults, Feb 11, 2026]** BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey 2026: use of ChatGPT and other generative AI for local recommendations rose "from 6% last year to 45%", making AI the third most popular recommendation source after Google (71%) and Facebook. 40% trust AI platforms for business recommendations; 82% read AI review summaries. https://brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/
2. **[Vendor stat, survey n=1,227 US consumers, July 9, 2026]** BrightLocal consumer search behavior: 23% used AI tools during their most recent local search, but only 8% chose AI as their first channel. 31% use AI for local recommendations at least monthly. Only 18% felt ready to contact an AI-recommended business without checking further; among AI-first searchers, 43% then checked Google and only 2% used AI alone. https://www.brightlocal.com/research/consumer-search-behavior-channels/

### What AI assistants cite for local recommendations
3. **[Vendor stat, 1.9M citations, 1,355 locations]** BrightLocal: GBP makes up 28.5% of all citations (present for 94.17% of businesses); Yelp 8.53% and dominant on ChatGPT (99,281 of 162,966 ChatGPT citations). BBB, Angi, and MapQuest are "regularly cited". About 93% of cited domains are individual business websites. Platforms: AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT. https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/ai-directory-sources/
4. **[Vendor stat, 6.8M citations, Jul-Aug 2025]** Yext: 86% of AI citations come from brand-controlled sources (websites 44%, listings 42%), reviews/social 8%, forums such as Reddit 2%. Gemini favors websites (52.1%), OpenAI favors listings (48.7%). Industries were retail, finance, healthcare, and food, not home services. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251009106549/en/Yext-Research-86-of-AI-Citations-Come-from-Brand-Managed-Sources-Clarifying-How-Marketers-Can-Compete-in-the-AI-Search-Era
5. **[Vendor stat via trade press, 28M+ AI responses, Q4 2025]** Foundation Marketing and AirOps, reported by PPC Land (May 28, 2026): Yelp had 512,680 AI citations, versus BBB 149,710, Angi 145,633, Thumbtack 56,004, HomeAdvisor 33,582, Nextdoor 10,308. That is a home-services-relevant directory set. https://ppc.land/yelp-gets-3-4x-more-ai-citations-than-any-rival-in-new-local-search-data/
6. **[Vendor stat reported by Search Engine Land, about 350K locations, 2,751 multi-location brands, Jan 28, 2026]** SOCi Local Visibility Index: share of locations recommended was ChatGPT 1.2%, Gemini 11%, Perplexity 7.4%, versus Google 3-pack 35.9%. AI visibility is "three to 30 times harder" to get. Fewer than half of Google local leaders are also AI leaders. ChatGPT-recommended locations averaged 4.3 stars versus 3.9 for Google local. https://searchengineland.com/ai-local-visibility-report-2026-468085
7. **[Vendor stat, one site's data]** Search Influence on Bing AI Performance: citations were highly concentrated (one page had 69% of citations). Anecdotal. https://www.searchinfluence.com/blog/bing-ai-performance-report-copilot-citations/

### SMB attitudes, spend, willingness to pay, churn
9. **[Vendor stat, n=350 US small businesses (1-50 employees), May 2026]** Bluehost: 87% are not acting on AI search optimization (13% are); 31% have heard of it but don't know how to proceed; 22% met the concept for the first time in the survey; 78% want to see how competitors appear in AI results; 47% would make a competitor ranking first in AI a top priority; 13% already get AI search traffic. https://www.bluehost.com/state-of-small-business-ai-confidence
10. **[Vendor stat, Clutch, Sept 24, 2025]** 60% of small businesses say AI search tools boost revenue and 44% report more leads, yet 57% still restrict AI crawler access. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250924544829/en
11. **[Vendor stat, n=7,413 UENI onboarding responses, Aug 2026; skews to very small or micro businesses]** Planned monthly marketing spend: $0 26.1%; $1-50 39.4%; $51-200 24.9%; $201-500 5.7%; $500+ 3.8%. 90.5% plan to spend under $200 a month. https://ueni.com/blog/small-business-marketing-budget/
