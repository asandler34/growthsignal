# Project brief

**Product:** GrowthSignal.ai, a self service AI search visibility product. Owner: Adam (GitHub `asandler34`).

**Customer:** owners of US single location local service businesses (home services first), and the web designers who build their sites (channel).

**Promise:** "See whether AI recommends your business, and exactly what to fix." Evidence plus fixes, not a tracking dashboard.

**Offer:** free instant scan (no account), free account with one real AI answer sample, then Check $9, Improve $29, Grow $59 a month.

**Positioning:** For owners of local service businesses and the web designers who look after their sites, GrowthSignal delivers a clear answer to "does AI recommend us, and what is stopping it?" plus the exact fixes, through a free instant readiness scan, repeated AI answer samples from official APIs, and copy and paste fixes built from facts the owner confirms, supported by a published rubric and method, every answer and source shown with uncertainty ranges, and no promises of placement.

## Rules that do not bend

- Never guarantee AI placement, indexing or recommendations.
- Readiness (controllable) and observed visibility (sampled) are separate results.
- Official APIs only; no scraping or automation of consumer AI apps.
- Sample reports are labeled as fictional. No invented logos, testimonials, metrics or outcomes. Borrowed quotes never read as testimonials.
- YC W19 and Harvard Launch Lab X belong to Adam's prior company Bensen and are labeled that way. No X.Wave branding.
- No real charges, live billing or DNS changes without Adam's explicit authorization.
- Secrets stay server side. Crawled content and model output are untrusted data.
- We never modify a customer's website.
